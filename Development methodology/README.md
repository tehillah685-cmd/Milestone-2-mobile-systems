**BIOPULSE DEVELOPMENT METHODOLOGY**

**Overview**

This folder contains project management artefacts used during the development of the BioPulse Monitoring System.

A Scrumban-inspired workflow was adopted to support both structured planning and continuous task management throughout the project. The workflow combined elements of Scrum and Kanban to manage UX artefact development, prototype design, evaluation activities, and final report preparation.
Scrum principles were applied during planning and iterative design activities, while Kanban principles were used to visualise work progression and manage individual tasks through Trello.

**Reflection**

The Trello board provided a clear visual representation of project progress and helped maintain organisation throughout development. As the sole contributor to the project, the board was particularly useful for tracking deliverables, monitoring priorities, and ensuring all milestone requirements were completed.

The workflow also supported iterative development, allowing design artefacts to evolve as new insights emerged during journey mapping, wireframing, prototyping, and evaluation.
